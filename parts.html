Print List

Six printed parts make up the robot. Everything else is off-the-shelf: two FS90R continuous servos, two 60 mm wheel hubs, two LM393 speed sensors, an IR reflective sensor, a 12 mm magnetic bead for the caster, a 4 × AA battery box, and the M5StickS3 on the Mini Bot HAT. Dimensions below come straight from the CAD model.

Print everything in PLA with 0.2 mm layers and 3 to 4 walls, except the tires, which are TPU for grip. The CAD model lists the tire as PETG only because Onshape has no TPU material. No supports are needed when each part sits on its flat face.

Looking for the step-by-step build guide? The Assembly page documents the previous version of the robot, built around the ESP32 Max V1 board. The parts on this page are for the current M5StickS3 build. Most steps still apply, but the board, HAT and printed parts differ.
